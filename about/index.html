About

Welcome! St. Louing is a personal project I created to document my experience living in St. Louis and learning to love it.

I've been on a quest to find the best food in St. Louis, and after realizing I've tried more St. Louis-style pizza than anyone I know, I thought a website would be a fun way to share everything I've discovered.

All photography on this site was taken in person, by me, in St. Louis.

Contact

Follow at st.louing or email me at hello@stlouing.com or fill out the form.

Uses

A few open-source projects this site relies on.

  • Host - Github Pages
  • Web Framework - Astro
  • Maps - MapLibre GL / Protomaps / OpenStreetMap
    • Map vector files for St. Louis greater area were saved as a .pmtiles binary and served from Supabase to prevent relying on third-party CDNs.
  • Icons - Tabler Icons
  • Forms - Formspree
  • Analytics - GoatCounter
